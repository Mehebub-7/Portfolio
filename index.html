About Me

Over the past 4 years I've shipped 50+ titles at UltraGames Entertainment and FlyingCaps Technologies, building core gameplay systems, editor tooling, and live-service integrations. 21 are featured below.

I gravitate toward problems where the right tool unlocks a bottleneck — a custom Unity editor that turned weeks of manual level authoring into a one-day job for the design team, and a reusable framework covering monetization, analytics and game-flow UI that new titles start from instead of rebuilding each time.

The other half of that is performance: cutting a build from 200 MB on Android to 20 MB on WebGL with Addressables and asset optimization, and keeping games running well on low-end hardware.
